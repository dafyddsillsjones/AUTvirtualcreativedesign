Coverage and limitations

This version includes the public VCDRC research index and people directory, with verified team relationships for selected projects. AUT academic profiles are linked where verified. Publication indexing is prepared but the complete AUT profile output lists have not yet been retrieved. Imported records are distinguished from project-team links. Do not interpret a missing link as evidence that a researcher has no publications or expertise.
